Protect Block Support

A reverse block-breaker: place blocks to protect your Life Wall from the enemy paddle and balls, and see how long you can survive.

FAQ

How do I play?
Drag one of the four blocks at the bottom and drop it below the dashed line. Tap a block to rotate it. Drop unwanted blocks in the trash, or keep one in the stock. The game ends when the Life Wall at the bottom reaches 0 HP.

How many times can I revive?
Up to twice per game by watching an ad at game over. Your Life Wall is fully restored.

My score is not on the leaderboard
Joining the ranking is off by default. Choose “Join the ranking” on the ranking screen or in Settings, and check that you are signed in under Settings → Game Center. Results are sent from your next game.

I don’t want my name on the leaderboard
Turn off “Join the ranking” in Settings and no results are sent. The name shown is your Game Center nickname, which you can change in Settings → Game Center.

How do I delete my records?
Deleting the App removes the records and settings stored on your device. Game Center records are managed by Apple.

There is no sound
The App stays silent when your iPhone is in silent mode. Also check Sound and Music in the pause menu.
