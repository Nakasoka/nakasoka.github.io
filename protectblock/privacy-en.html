Privacy Policy

Protect Block (“the App”) / Operator: Nakasoka / Effective: November 5, 2026

The App can be played without an account. The operator does not collect your name, email address or similar personal information. This policy explains what information the App handles and what is sent to third parties to show ads.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Your best record, the on-device ranking, and your sound, music, vibration and language settings
  • If you have joined the ranking: results that could not be sent to Game Center because you were offline (they are sent to Game Center the next time you are online, then deleted)

This stays on your device and is never sent to the operator. Deleting the App deletes it.

2. Game Center (leaderboards)

Joining the ranking is optional and is off by default. Only if you choose “Join the ranking” in Settings or on the ranking screen, and are signed in to Game Center, is your result (survival time) sent to Apple’s Game Center leaderboards.

  • When you join, your Game Center nickname and records are shown to other players on the ranking.
  • Only your Game Center nickname is shown, never your real name or email address. You can change who sees your profile in Settings → Game Center.
  • You can leave the ranking at any time in Settings. After that, no results are sent (results already sent remain on Game Center).

Game Center information such as your nickname is managed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad impressions, views and taps, and how you use the App
  • Diagnostic information about errors and performance

See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Tracking and ad choices

  • On first launch the App asks whether you allow tracking for ads. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In regions that require consent (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), a consent form is shown at launch. You can change your choice from “Ad settings” on the title screen.

6. Children

The App is not designed for children.

7. Disclosure to third parties

Except as required by law, the operator does not provide your information to third parties (other than the advertising and Game Center transmissions described above).
